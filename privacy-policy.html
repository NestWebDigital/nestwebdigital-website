To exercise any of these rights, contact us at NestWebDigitalofficial@hotmail.com.

7. Cookies

This site uses a small number of cookies and similar technologies:

  • Essential: a local storage entry that remembers your cookie preference (accepted or essential-only), so you're not asked again on every visit.
  • Payments (PayPal): when you interact with a payment or subscription button, PayPal may set cookies to process the transaction and prevent fraud.
  • Booking (Calendly): opening the booking popup may set cookies used to manage your scheduling session.
  • Contact form (Formspree): submitting the contact form may set a cookie used for spam protection.
  • Fonts (Google Fonts): this site loads fonts from Google's servers, which involves your browser contacting Google directly; no cookie is set by this on our end, but Google may process your IP address as part of serving the font files.

You can choose "Essential only" in the cookie banner to decline the non-essential cookies described above where technically possible. Declining does not disable PayPal or Calendly's own cookies if you actively choose to use those features, since they are required for those services to function.

We may update this policy from time to time; the "last updated" date above will reflect the latest version.
